# Cutline privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Cutline is made by Maverick Smith.

## Your videos stay on your phone

Cutline reads the speech in a video on the device itself to write captions. The video, its sound and the captions are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Cutline has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

## Advertising

Cutline shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect the device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about how ads are used. Where the law requires it, Cutline asks for your choice first, and you can change it at any time in Settings under Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google explains how it uses this data at <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and handled by Google Play. Cutline only learns whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

## Updates

Cutline checks whether a newer version exists. That check sends no personal information.

## Children

Cutline is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Drafts are stored only on your phone. Deleting a draft, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Cutline removes them. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
